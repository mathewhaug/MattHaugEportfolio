Teaching Philosophy

I believe adult learners thrive when programming is taught with clarity, purpose, and relevance. With over 10 years of industry experience and 4 years in the classroom, I focus on what matters most: building confidence through hands on coding and real-world problem solving.

My teaching style is direct and supportive, I break down complex concepts into practical, understandable steps, helping students apply their knowledge immediately in meaningful ways.

My goal is to prepare learners to think critically, troubleshoot independently, and build the confidence to code professionally in any environment.
